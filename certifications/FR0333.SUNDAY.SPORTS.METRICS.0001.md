## METRICS PINS
PIN.SPORTS.0001 NUMBER.ALONE.NOT.STATISTIC: require observation, value, reference point, delta, direction, context, source, timestamp and sample size.
PIN.SPORTS.0002 ODDS.TIME.LOCK: opening, closing and in-play prices must never be mixed; absent timestamp -> unknown.
PIN.SPORTS.0003 NO.LOOKAHEAD: Sunday outcome cannot be used as a Sunday pregame prediction; train/test chronologically.
PIN.SPORTS.0004 CALIBRATE: log loss, Brier score, calibration bins, sample sizes and uncertainty.
PIN.SPORTS.0005 MARKET.VIG: convert American odds to implied probabilities, remove overround for comparable markets before calculating model edge.
PIN.SPORTS.0006 BETTING.RISK: simulated bets only until independent validation; record ROI, max drawdown, confidence intervals, and avoid profitability claims.
PIN.SPORTS.0007 TWO.LANES: 16.P passive source ingestion and 18.R active replay/backtest; neither self-certifies.
PIN.SPORTS.0008 EVIDENCE.GATE: preserve all misses, no cherry-picking, source discrepancies HOLD.

## MODEL EVALUATION
Historical betting odds are not proof of predictive advantage. Compare a simple baseline with a candidate on games strictly after training period. Assess probability calibration, log loss, Brier score, closing-line value only if closing price is independently timestamped, and simulated return net of vig. Do not promise profitable bets.
