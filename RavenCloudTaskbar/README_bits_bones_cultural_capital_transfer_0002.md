## Purpose

This rail measures where creative contribution and downstream commercial reward diverge. It is designed to distinguish documented structural exploitation from ordinary cultural exchange, influence, cover recording, or later popularization.

## Core pipeline

`CREATION -> OWNERSHIP -> DISTRIBUTION -> MARKET.TRANSLATION -> VISUAL.IDENTITY -> REWARD -> RECOGNITION -> LEGACY`

## Creation field map

- COMPOSITION: lyric or melodic blueprint.
- PERFORMANCE: vocal delivery, improvisation, and melodic realization.
- ARRANGEMENT: tempo, meter, rhythmic style, and structural alteration.
- PRODUCTION: sonic texture, tracking environment, and backing execution.

These fields remain separate because legal authorship does not exhaust creative contribution.

## Evidence boundaries

Financial and historical claims must carry an evidence class. Audited records may establish ledger facts. Historically corroborated recollections may support guarded facts but cannot be promoted to audited lifetime royalty totals. Public anecdotes remain narrative data. Primary or institutional histories are authoritative only for the scope they document.

Canonical terminal states remain `T.20`, `U.21`, and `F.6`. Subcodes may refine a terminal state but do not replace it.

## Market translation gate

A later recording may reformat an existing song for another audience without becoming a new composition. The rail tracks original performance, cover adaptation, format compatibility, radio acceptability, retail acceptability, crossover access, and market outcome.

`MARKET.TRANSLATION != NEW.COMPOSITION`

## Visual identity gate

The visual packaging layer measures whether the performer is shown, what substitute subject appears, whether race is visible, the documented or inferred marketing intent, and the apparent target market.

The rail does not infer racist intent from an image alone. `LABEL.MARKETING.INTENT = T.20` requires an executive statement, label record, or equivalent strong source.

`AUDIO.IDENTITY != VISUAL.IDENTITY`

`PERFORMER.PRESENT != PERFORMER.VISIBLE`

## Case 0001 Hound Dog

The rail preserves the difference between legal authorship and performance origin. Big Mama Thornton is recorded as the original major performer while Jerry Leiber and Mike Stoller remain the legal authors. The case supports structural commercial divergence while rejecting a direct composition-theft claim against Elvis Presley without direct evidence.

## Case 0002 Tutti Frutti

The rail records Little Richard's original performance and pop crossover, Pat Boone's stronger pop-chart peak, the market-translation layer, source-sensitive authorship metadata, and a hold on any exact artist-earnings delta that is not supported by an audited ledger.

`STRUCTURAL.BARRIER != ABSOLUTE.BARRIER`

A creator can break through a discriminatory structure without disproving that the structure existed.

## Historical packaging layer

The specification distinguishes 1920s catalog segregation from later album-cover representation. Race and hillbilly catalog categories are treated as a distribution taxonomy. Later visual packaging is measured asset by asset. Intent remains documented, inferred, or unknown rather than being guessed.
