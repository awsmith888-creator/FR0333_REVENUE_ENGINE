## Purpose

This index connects two evolution rails without collapsing them into one claim:

1. Motorcycle evolution from early mass production through sport, touring, rider-fit engineering, electronic control, freestyle competition, and modification economics.
2. AI system evolution from rule-based systems through deep learning, transformers, foundation models, reasoning, tool use, agents, longer context, faster inference, and lower cost per unit of useful work.

The comparison is structural, not literal. A motorcycle and an AI system do not evolve biologically and a running AI model instance does not autonomously rewrite itself every day.

## Governing distinction

The user's core intuition is preserved as a system-level continuum: AI products and model families can continue to advance through new training runs, architectures, tools, infrastructure, evaluation, safety work, and releases.

The evidence boundary is equally important:

`MODEL.INSTANCE.SELF.MODIFICATION = FALSE`

`SYSTEM.EVOLUTION = TRAINING + RELEASES + TOOLING + INFRASTRUCTURE + FEEDBACK`

`CURRENT.TREND != GUARANTEED.FUTURE.RATE`

So the defensible statement is not that one running assistant silently changes itself every day. The defensible statement is that the surrounding model family and product system have shown repeated measurable capability, efficiency, tool-use, context, and deployment improvements, and future releases may continue that continuum.

## Shared architecture law

`EVOLUTION = CORE.MACHINE + CONTROL.SYSTEM + USER.FIT + FEEDBACK + SAFETY + SPECIALIZATION`

For motorcycles, the core machine is the engine/frame platform and the control system increasingly includes ABS, IMUs, traction control, and rider aids.

For AI, the core machine is the trained model and the control system includes inference infrastructure, reasoning modes, tools, agent harnesses, evaluation, and safety layers.

## Evidence boundary

Motorcycle values are preserved from the current research baseline. AI trend values are separated into independent public research and first-party OpenAI product data. Future capability growth remains `UNVERIFIED.FUTURE.CLAIM.HOLD` until observed.
