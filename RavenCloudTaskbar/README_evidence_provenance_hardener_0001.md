# FR0333.EVIDENCE.PROVENANCE.HARDENER.0001

## Purpose

Harden claim intake so a social-media observation can start a research lane without becoming ground truth by repetition, popularity, or rhetorical force.

## Governing axiom

`OBSERVED != CORRELATED != CAUSAL`

Every promoted claim must preserve its evidence relation. A current disparity does not by itself prove a single historical cause. A causal claim requires causal evidence and bounded scope.

## Source ladder

1. `PRIMARY.GOVERNMENT`
2. `PRIMARY.INTERGOVERNMENTAL`
3. `PRIMARY.INSTITUTIONAL`
4. `PEER_REVIEWED`
5. `INDEPENDENT.SECONDARY`

Source quantity does not substitute for source independence. Two pages derived from the same upstream record remain one independence group.

## Claim contract

`CLAIM + TYPE + SCOPE + SOURCE + INDEPENDENCE + STATE + RECEIPT`

Truth states remain:

- `T.20` documented or bounded causal support
- `U.21` unresolved, incomplete, or awaiting sufficient evidence
- `F.6` false or unsupported under the stated scope

## Absolute-language gate

Claims containing universal or monocausal terms such as `ALL`, `EVERY`, `ONLY`, `ENTIRELY`, or `SOLELY` cannot enter `T.20` merely because the underlying topic is historically well supported. The exact absolute proposition must itself be established. Otherwise it remains `U.21` or `F.6`.

Examples held at `F.6` in the v3 transaction:

- all Western wealth derives from slavery
- every current racial disparity is caused solely by slavery
- all Black identity was erased

## Legal-effect gate

A recorded vote and a legal effect are separate claims.

For UN General Assembly Resolution `A/RES/80/250`:

- adoption and vote count are source-bound observations
- sovereign-state names are normalized
- `JERUSALEM` is not accepted as a substitute for the voting state `ISRAEL`
- the resolution is represented as a non-binding General Assembly resolution
- adoption does not become a binding individualized cash award

## Bounded-causality gate

A `T.20` causal claim requires:

- `evidence_relation = CAUSAL`
- `causal_scope = BOUNDED`
- at least one peer-reviewed source receipt
- scope no broader than the study or evidence supports

The exact total share of present-day Black-White disparities attributable specifically to slavery remains `U.21` unless a defensible quantitative attribution model is supplied.

## Historical evidence examples bound in v3

The v3 transaction binds primary or peer-reviewed records from the United Nations, University College London, Harvard University, CARICOM, the African Union, the U.S. Federal Reserve, the U.S. Census Bureau, the American Sociological Review, and the American Economic Review.

These records support discrete claims concerning recorded reparations policy, slave-owner compensation, institutional slavery ties, contemporary wealth and income disparities, long-run redlining effects, and bounded long-run slave-trade effects. They do not authorize universal or monocausal claims beyond their scopes.

## Human promotion boundary

`AUTOMATIC.PROMOTION = FALSE`

`CANONICAL.PROMOTION = U.21.HUMAN.REVIEW.REQUIRED`

`HUMANLOCK = ACTIVE`

Structural validation may open the hardening route. It does not authorize a canonical historical, legal, political, or causal conclusion without human review.

On CHOMP CHOMP:

- preserve source set
- preserve count
- preserve order
- preserve active aspect ratio
- do not generate
- do not reinterpret
- do not expand the branch
- require a new explicit instruction before mutation
