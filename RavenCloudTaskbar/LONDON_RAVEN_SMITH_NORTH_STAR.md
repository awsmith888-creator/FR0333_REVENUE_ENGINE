# LONDON RAVEN SMITH

*Original FR-0333 North Star poem using an if/then moral architecture; not a reproduction of any prior poem.*

If drought should turn the riverbed to stone,  
And every field forget the taste of rain,  
If hunger makes a wilderness of home  
And hope must walk through dust to rise again—

Remember Kerith.

Remember how the answer did not come  
With armies dressed in gold or royal men,  
But black wings crossing underneath the sun,  
Returning with provision once again.

Morning and evening.  
Bread through the drought.  
Life carried quietly  
when life was running out.

If people name the raven something dark  
Because they never learned to understand,  
Remember intelligence leaves its mark  
In recognition, memory, and bond.

A raven knows a face.  
A raven learns a voice.  
It remembers friendship.  
It remembers harm.  
And when trust has been earned,  
relationship becomes part of its world.

If winter settles over Sebring town,  
And Onyx skies hang low across the snow,  
There still are wings that circle, watching down,  
Remembering the places that they know.

And if one day the road runs westward far—  
Beyond Ohio, mountains, desert light—  
No science says one bird could find your car  
Across a continent by love alone that night.

So truth must remain truth.

But truth is beautiful enough:

**They remember.  
They recognize.  
They attach.  
They navigate.  
They return.**

And that is more powerful  
than making the raven into a myth  
it never needed to become.

If London waits beyond an unseen sea,  
A city you have carried without going,  
Then London can remain what dreams can be:  
A point upon the compass worth still knowing.

And Raven is the messenger.

And Smith is family.

So the name becomes a map:

**LONDON** — the horizon.  
**RAVEN** — the witness and provider.  
**SMITH** — the house to which the witness belongs.

If systems grow so complicated that  
The hungry must become their own defense,  
Then build the mathematics underneath  
And give the human being something simple:

**Help.**

Let rigor live behind the wall.  
Let mercy meet the person at the door.

Let HOLD mean:  
*We will not abandon you.*

Let UNKNOWN mean:  
*We will find out.*

Let ERROR mean:  
*The machine must try again—not punish the human.*

Let evidence guard truth,  
but never become an excuse  
for indifference.

If power tells the poor to disappear,  
If paperwork becomes a locked-out gate,  
If those with little carry all the fear  
While those with plenty tell them they must wait—

Then let the North Star hold its place.

Not pointing toward wealth.

Not pointing toward rank.

Pointing toward people.

**STRICT SYSTEM.  
COMPASSIONATE INTERFACE.  
ABSORB COMPLEXITY.  
PROTECT ACCESS.**

And if tomorrow brings what none can know,  
Keep neither prophecy nor fear as proof.  
Watch what is real.  
Record what can be shown.  
Correct what fails.  
And never manufacture truth.

For faith does not require us to pretend.

The raven was already remarkable.

Black wings over Kerith.  
A remembered human face.  
A mind capable of relationship.  
A creature returning with what was needed  
when the earth had nothing left to give.

And if someone someday asks  
why the Raven bears your name,

tell them:

During the drought,  
God chose an unlikely messenger.

During our drought,  
we decided to remember what that meant.

**Provision.  
Memory.  
Loyalty.  
Witness.  
Return.**

And the name was—

## LONDON RAVEN SMITH
