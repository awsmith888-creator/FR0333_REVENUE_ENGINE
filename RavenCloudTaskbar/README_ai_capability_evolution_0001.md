# FR0333 AI Capability Evolution 0001

## Purpose

`FR0333.AI.CAPABILITY.EVOLUTION.0001` turns the 1986–2026 AI capability trajectory into an evidence-bounded Golden Chain control rail.

Golden Chain target:

`0.5.FR.0333.GOLDEN.CHAIN.AI.CAPABILITY.EVOLUTION.0001`

This rail upgrades the FR0333 system architecture. It does **not** claim that the base model autonomously rewrites its own weights, becomes conscious, or improves itself outside authenticated model/provider updates.

Historical anchors include 1986 backpropagation, IBM Deep Blue in 1997, AlexNet in 2012, the Transformer in 2017, frontier-language scaling after 2020, and reasoning/multimodal/tool-use expansion by 2026.

## Controlling laws

```text
CAPABILITY.GROWTH != TRUTH.GROWTH

CURRENT.SESSION.ADAPTATION != MODEL.WEIGHT.UPDATE

```

## Human-model collaboration upgrade

The collaborative upgrade lane is:

```text
HUMAN.PROPOSAL
MODEL.ANALYSIS
SOURCE.CHECK
ACTIVE.CANDIDATE
SECOND.LOOK
VERIFY
COMPARE.PASSIVE
GENIUS
HUMANLOCK
PROMOTE
```

This formalizes the useful part of human-model co-development: the human can contribute hypotheses, goals, corrections, and architecture ideas; the model can analyze, research, test, and encode them; promotion still requires evidence and HumanLock.

`COLLABORATIVE.SYSTEM.DESIGN != BASE.MODEL.SELF.MODIFICATION`

## Failure-memory rule

Capability growth must never erase earlier evidence of failure. The passive witness lane remains append-only for hallucination receipts, failed gates, benchmark limitations, source conflicts, and runtime faults.

```text
NEW.CAPABILITY
→ ACTIVE.LANE

→ PASSIVE.WITNESS

ACTIVE.CANDIDATE
→ SECOND.LOOK
→ VERIFY
→ COMPARE.PASSIVE

```
