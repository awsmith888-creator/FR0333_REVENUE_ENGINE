## Evidence boundaries / unresolved questions

- **ALLIANCE CRITICISM != ALLIANCE BREAK:** Surveyed public confidence, government votes, and actual defense/diplomatic cooperation are different measures. No evidence here demonstrates that NATO is abandoning its U.S. commitments or that countries are preparing collective hostilities against Americans.
- **OFAC LICENSE != PRESIDENTIAL PURCHASE != PERSONAL FINANCIAL BENEFIT:** Do not conflate these. No source in this record establishes a personal diesel-related payment to the president.
- **STATUTE + LICENSE != FINAL COURT HOLDING:** The two primary documents create a legal question requiring statutory, regulatory and procedural analysis. Do not assert a final illegality ruling without a case, holding and disposition.
- **POLL ≠ ELECTION RESULT ≠ CAUSATION:** Give field dates, population, sample size and methodology. Never transform disapproval into a forecast or infer motivations without evidence.
- **PARTISAN STATEMENTS != DOCUMENTED INTENT:** Attribute commentators' accusations and compare exact wording and dates. Do not convert inconsistency into proven knowing deception without evidence.
- **PLEDGE != DELIVERY:** NATO's pledged level should not be described as already transferred in full.
- Previous conversational assertions about fuel volumes, political motives, exact quotes, or alleged profits that are not individually sourced above remain **HOLD**, not promoted as findings.

## Safe use in Sophie civic education

1. Present a source, its issuing institution, publication date, observation period and **exact metric or legal clause**.
2. Teach learners to distinguish **documented action**, **reported interpretation**, **legal claim**, **court holding** and **unresolved question**.
3. For comparisons, retain population and denominator; compare like with like.
4. For legal studies, quote statutes or holdings narrowly; include the strongest competing legal interpretation before declaring a conclusion.
5. Require human editorial review before displaying political or legal teaching materials to children or making a public release.
