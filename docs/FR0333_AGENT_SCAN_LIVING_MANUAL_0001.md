## Standard investigation procedure
1. DISCOVER: identify official product/API documentation and date.
2. SOURCE: record canonical URL, publisher, publication or access date, evidence class.
3. CAPABILITY: distinguish marketing, demo, generally available service, and deployed system.
4. ACCESS: determine API/connector, authorization, cost, quotas, region, and limitations.
5. TEST: design bounded test; do not execute external integrations without approval.
6. RECEIPT: preserve test inputs, timestamps, outputs, errors, and version.
7. DELTA: compare against current FR-0333 runtime baseline.
8. HUMANLOCK: HOLD until evidence and explicit authorization.
9. STAY: append outcomes without deleting earlier states.
